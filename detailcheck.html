Proofing · a final set of eyes

Errors I caught on live sites.

Part of good copy is catching the small things before they ship. These are real mistakes sitting on live Atlanta business pages right now, each with the fix.

House of Wellness Med Spa
Footer copyright
Found© 2035 by The Health Spa
Fix© 2026 The House of Wellness Med Spa

Wrong year and the wrong business name, on the page asking you to trust them with your skin.

Natural Body Spa
Location count
Found"Six locations" up top, "all five of our locations" lower down
FixPick the true number and use it everywhere. It's six.

A contradiction on one page makes a reader wonder what else is sloppy.

Natural Body Spa
Promo banner
Found"Black Friday is December 28th, 2025"
FixBlack Friday 2025 was November 28. Correct it or pull the banner.

A wrong, expired promo date tells visitors nobody is watching the site.

Accupac
Footer and body
FoundCopyright still reads © 2023, with a wall of keyword spam in the body
FixUpdate the year and cut the keyword list.

A stale year plus visible SEO stuffing makes a premium maker look cheap to the exact brands they want.
