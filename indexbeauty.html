Homepage Rewrite

Prepared for The Index Beauty · The ATL Edit
A note on your site

I found The Index Beauty while hunting down indie Atlanta skincare and candle makers, which means your directory is already doing its job. People like me land here. But the copy is quiet. It describes the scene beautifully and then stops. It never tells a shopper why to buy indie and local, and it never tells a founder why to get listed.

This rewrite keeps your editorial tone and adds the pushes that turn a browser into a subscriber and a founder into a submission.

The core problem

Your site has two jobs: get shoppers to discover and buy from these brands, and get founders to submit theirs. The current copy does neither hard. The hero, "Indie Beauty in Atlanta, The ATL edit," is elegant but passive. The about paragraph describes the scene without giving anyone a reason to act, and the newsletter ask is a generic "stay in the loop." This rewrite adds a point of view and a reason to click, for both the people shopping and the brands that want to be found.

Hero Headline
Current
Indie Beauty in Atlanta. The ATL edit.
Rewritten
The beauty brands Atlanta makes by hand. All in one place.
Keeps your editorial feel but adds the promise, handmade and local, and the benefit, you don't have to hunt for them. "The ATL edit" can stay as the tagline underneath.
About / Value Prop
Current
Atlanta is home to a growing community of independent beauty founders producing handmade skincare, body care, candles, and fragrance in small, carefully sourced batches. The Index Beauty tracks every indie brand worth knowing across the Atlanta area.
Rewritten
The big brands, you already know. This is everyone else. The skincare mixed in a Westside studio, the candles poured in East Atlanta, the fragrance blended in small batches by founders who answer their own emails. We find the Atlanta indie brands worth your money and put them in one place, so your next favorite isn't buried in a hashtag.
Turns a description into a reason to care. It contrasts indie with mass market, makes "local" specific and tangible, and ends on the real benefit: discovery without the endless scroll.
For Shoppers (new section)
Current
None. Shoppers just see a category list.
Rewritten
Shop small, on purpose. Every brand here is independent, Atlanta based, and made in small batches. Your money goes to a person, not a shareholder, and you walk away with something nobody else in the room is wearing.
Gives the shopper the emotional reason to choose indie. Right now the site assumes they already want to. This makes the case.
For Founders / Submit
Current
Submit a brand. (A nav link with no pitch behind it.)
Rewritten
Make what you make? Get found. If you're an Atlanta beauty founder, a listing puts you in front of shoppers and press who are here specifically to discover brands like yours. Free to submit. We review every one.
Your "Submit" link has no pitch behind it. Founders need to know what they get: the right audience, visibility, and that it's free. This turns a quiet link into a reason to apply.
Newsletter
Current
Stay in the loop. The monthly edit from The Index Beauty.
Rewritten
One email a month. The new brands we found, the ones worth your money, and where to get them before everyone else does.
"Stay in the loop" asks for an email and gives nothing back. This tells them exactly what they'll get and adds a little urgency with "before everyone else."
