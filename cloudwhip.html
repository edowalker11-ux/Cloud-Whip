The difference

A rich butter that acts like a light lotion.

Most body butters make you pick a side. Thick ones that fix dry skin but leave you shiny and stuck to your clothes, or light ones that feel nice for an hour and quit.

Cloud Whip doesn't make you choose. We whip shea, mango, and cocoa butter into a cloud that melts the second it hits warm skin, so it absorbs fast and still holds moisture for a full day. You get the fix of a rich butter with the feel of a light lotion.

Scoop it on after a shower, work it into the spots that go dry first, and feel the difference before you finish the other leg.

How to use

Scoop

A small amount with clean, dry fingers. A little goes far.

Warm

Rub between your palms for a second. It melts right away.

Massage

Into damp or dry skin. Focus on elbows, knees, and heels.

Daily

Best right after a shower, while skin is still a little damp.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Is it greasy?

No. It's whipped light so it sinks in fast. Give it thirty seconds and you can get dressed with no film.

Will it clog pores?

It's made for body skin and tested non comedogenic. For your face, patch test first.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. The Bare option is fragrance free, and the whole line is dermatologist tested.

Does a little really last 24 hours?

One scoop holds moisture for a full day on most skin. Very dry skin may want a second layer on knees and heels.

Is it vegan?

Yes, and cruelty free. No animal ingredients, no animal testing.

How long does a jar last?

Daily full body use runs about six to eight weeks.
