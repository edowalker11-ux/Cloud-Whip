Homepage Rewrite

Prepared for Highland Pet Supply · Virginia Highland, Atlanta
A note on your site

I found Highland while looking for the best independent pet shops in Atlanta, and your Virginia Highland spot has a great reputation. But your homepage doesn't show any of that. It opens with curbside pickup instructions from a few years back and a gift card offer, then points people to a Yahoo address. A first time visitor has no idea you have a training center, curated food, or staff who actually know pets.

This rewrite fixes that. Same shop, same heart, just copy that finally sells what makes you worth the drive.

The core problem

Your page leads with logistics, curbside pickup and gift cards, not a single reason to choose you over Chewy or a big box store. Your real edge is hiding in one line of your welcome text: you have both a training center and a retail location. That is the whole pitch. A neighborhood shop with expert staff and a trainer in the building is something no warehouse can offer, and right now it's an afterthought. This rewrite pulls it to the front.

Headline
Current
Welcome to Highland Pet Supply! This is Atlanta's Highland Pet Supply on the web.
Rewritten
Good food, real advice, and a trainer who knows your dog.
"Welcome to our website" is wasted space. The new headline names your three advantages in seven words, and every one of them is something Chewy cannot do.
Subhead
Current
None. The page jumps straight into curbside pickup instructions.
Rewritten
Highland Pet Supply is Atlanta's family run pet store and training center in Virginia Highland. Everything your pet needs, picked by people who actually use it.
Gives a first time visitor the who, what, and where in one breath, and plants the local, expert angle before they scroll.
Why Highland (new section)
Current
None. Your page never says why to choose you.
Rewritten
We know pets, not just products. Ask us anything and get a straight answer, not a shrug. A training center, right here. Puppy basics to problem behavior, in the same place you grab the treats. Local beats a warehouse. Out of food on a Sunday? Walk in. No shipping wait, no subscription you forgot to cancel.
Three short reasons to pick you, each aimed straight at the online competition. This is the section your current page is missing entirely, and it's the one that actually sells the store.
Pickup Section
Current
Now offering curbside, no-touch pick-up! During this difficult time, Highland Pet Supply is staying open to ensure you can get food and supplies for your furry family members...
Rewritten
Short on time? Call or email your order and pay online. We'll have it bagged and loaded before you park.
Your curbside copy still reads like 2020. Same service, reframed as everyday convenience instead of a pandemic measure.
Gift Cards
Current
Gift cards available for all the pets in your life! Highland Pet Supply offers online gift cards in any denomination... Learn more, click here.
Rewritten
Give the pet lover in your life exactly what they want. Any amount, emailed the same day.
Shorter, warmer, and it drops the clunky "click here."
Closing Call to Action
Current
Hours and a Yahoo email buried at the bottom of the page.
Rewritten
Come see us in Virginia Highland. 1186 North Highland Avenue NE. Open 7 days, staffed by people who'll remember your dog's name. Your pet will thank you.
Ends with a clear, local invitation and one more hit of the personal touch, instead of trailing off into store hours.
