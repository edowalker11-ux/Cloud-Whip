How it works

Satin does the work while you sleep.

Hot tools give you curls and take your hair's health as payment. Heatless sets usually mean lumpy, half held waves by lunch.

Beauty Sleep is a satin wrapped rod, so there's nothing to snag, pull, or fry. You wrap damp hair around it at night, and the satin holds the shape without friction while you sleep. Unwrap in the morning for soft, even waves that last all day. No heat, no damage, no five am curling iron.

How to use

Damp

Start with damp, detangled hair. Not soaking.

Wrap

Rest the rod behind your head and wrap sections around it.

Secure

Tie off the ends with the satin scrunchies.

Sleep

Unwrap in the morning and shake out your waves.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Wet or dry hair?

Damp works best. Towel dried, not soaking. Soaking wet hair won't set by morning.

How long do the curls last?

A full day on most hair, longer with a little hairspray or a light mousse before wrapping.

Does it work on short hair?

Yes, down to about chin length. Shorter hair gets a soft wave rather than a full curl.

Will it damage my hair?

No heat and a satin surface means no breakage or frizz from friction. That's the whole point.

How do I wash it?

Hand wash the satin cover in cool water and lay flat to dry.
