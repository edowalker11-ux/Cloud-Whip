Homepage Rewrite

Prepared for The House of Wellness Med Spa · Downtown Atlanta
A note on your site

I came across House of Wellness while looking at downtown Atlanta spas, and the setup is strong: head spa, massages, facials, right by the stadiums and GSU. But your homepage doesn't lead with any of it. It opens with your address and a list of nearby landmarks, and the footer still reads "© 2035 by The Health Spa," which isn't even your name.

Small thing, but to someone deciding whether to trust you with their skin, it reads as unfinished. This rewrite puts your best services up front and cleans up the details.

The core problem

Your page opens with a map and a list of nearby stadiums, not a reason to book. The most searched, most booked service you offer, the head spa, sits fourth on a plain bullet list, and your downtown location is framed as directions instead of a selling point. On top of that, the footer copyright reads 2035 and "The Health Spa." A visitor deciding whether to spend money on their face notices things like that. This rewrite leads with what people actually want and fixes the trust leaks.

Headline
Current
None. The page opens with "Location / 18 Marietta St NW."
Rewritten
Your downtown Atlanta reset. Massages, facials, and the head spa everyone's booking.
Right now there's no headline at all, just an address. This gives a visitor an instant reason to keep reading and leads with your most in demand service.
Subhead
Current
Our team of licensed wellness & beauty specialists provides: Massages, Hair Salon Services, Facials, Vitamin Shots, Head Spa.
Rewritten
Licensed specialists, a private relaxation lounge, and same day appointments, steps from Mercedes-Benz Stadium and State Farm Arena.
Keeps your service range but adds the two things that close a booking: same day availability and a premium setting. The landmarks become a convenience perk, not the opening line.
Head Spa Spotlight (new section)
Current
Listed as one bullet among five services.
Rewritten
New here for the head spa? Good call. A deep cleanse, scalp massage, and warm rinse that leaves your hair and your head feeling brand new. The treatment people are driving across the city for.
The head spa is a trend people are actively searching for. Giving it its own moment turns curiosity into a booking instead of hiding it in a list.
Perfect For
Current
A relaxing reset in the city. A mini makeover before an event. Health, wellness, and beauty services that help you look & feel your best.
Rewritten
Here for a reset between meetings, a glow up before the game, or an hour that's finally just for you. Walk out refreshed, confident, and cared for.
Your current version is close. This makes the occasions concrete and ties them to your downtown, event driven crowd.
Booking
Current
If we don't answer immediately, it simply means we're servicing a client and protecting the peaceful environment they deserve... We can't wait to service you.
Rewritten
Book online any time, or text us. If we don't pick up, we're with a client protecting the calm you're coming in for. Leave a text and we'll get right back to you.
Keeps your gracious tone but leads with the easiest action, booking online, and trims the repetition. "Service you" reads a little off for a spa; "get right back to you" is warmer.
Footer (trust fix)
Current
© 2035 by The Health Spa.
Rewritten
© 2026 The House of Wellness Med Spa · Downtown Atlanta.
A two second fix that removes a quiet red flag. The wrong year and the wrong business name make a careful customer wonder what else is unfinished.
